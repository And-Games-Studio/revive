ReVive

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: ReVive is free to play. It shows ads through Google AdMob, and some items in its shop can be bought with real money through Google Play. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads use your personal data, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. There are no accounts: your town and your progress stay on your phone. The game sends anonymous play data (which rooms you visit, how long steps take, what you decorate) to Unity Analytics only if you say yes to "Help us improve"; you can change that in Settings → Share play data. The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who we are

ReVive is made by Andrea Ebano, publishing as And Games, based in Portugal. Andrea Ebano is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com.

What data the game uses, and why

Playing

Your town's name, your progress (the places you've restored, down to each object), your decorations, coins, daily tasks and gifts, your album's photos and your settings are stored on your phone only. They are never sent anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are shown by Google AdMob, a Google service. To show and measure them, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game sends Google:

  • your phone's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings, under Google → Ads);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country, region) can be worked out;
  • information about your device and the app: model, operating system and its language, the app's version;
  • how you interact with the ads: which ones were shown, whether you watched or tapped them;
  • your consent choices, where you were asked.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google appears the first time you open the game. It lists the purposes and the ad technology providers that work with Google; ads are personalised, and information is stored on or read from your phone for ads, only if you agree. If you don't, you still see ads, but they aren't based on your personal data. You can change your choice whenever you like in the game's Settings. The game asks Google only for ads rated suitable for most audiences. Google uses this data as described in its own policies, including how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We never see your advertising ID or the other data Google receives; we only get totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Purchases (Google Play)

Some items in the game's shop can be bought with real money. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your card or other payment details. Google Play tells the game what you bought, so it can give it to you, and the game keeps that on your phone. The purchasing library the game uses to talk to Google Play may also report each purchase (the product, its price, when it was bought, the store's receipt, and basic details of your phone and the game) to Unity Technologies, which processes it for us so we can see sales. Google handles your purchase as its own privacy policy describes.

Play data (Unity Analytics), only if you say yes

The first time you play, the game asks whether you want to help us improve it. If you say yes, it sends Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, events about how you play:

  • the places you enter and leave, how long you stay and how many objects you finish;
  • each restoration step you complete and how long it took, the objects and places you finish, and when you use a hint or follow a tip of the first-time guide;
  • the decorations you place and buy, the theme packs you open, the rare decorations you win and the daily tasks you claim;
  • which tabs you open, and that you shared a photo (not the photo);
  • the game's frame rate in each place.

With these, Unity's software sends a random identifier it creates for this installation of the game, when you play (sessions), your phone's model, operating system and language, the game's version, and your IP address, from which Unity works out your country. These events contain only identifiers and numbers: never your town's name, nothing you type, and nothing that tells us who you are. We use them to see where players get stuck or give up, and what to improve. If you say no, or never answer, nothing is sent. If you switch Share play data off later, sending stops at once.

Sharing a photo

"Share" in the album passes the before and after picture of a place, with a short text, to the app you pick. We don't see whom you send it to.

Technical data

Google (for ads and purchases) and Unity receive your IP address and basic technical information when the game connects to them, to make the connection work and keep the services secure. The Unity engine, which the game is made with, may also send Unity anonymous information about your device (model, operating system, graphics hardware) when the game starts.

Legal bases

Purchases are handled to give you what you bought (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). Ads keep the game free: personalised ads, and storing or reading information on your phone for ads, rest on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the game's Settings; ads that aren't personalised, their measurement and fraud prevention rest on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)), which you can object to in the same place. Play data is sent only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings → Share play data.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Play data is kept in our Unity Analytics project only as long as we need it to improve the game, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Your consent choices for ads stay on your phone until you change them or clear the game's data; Google keeps ad data for the periods set out in its privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com; to delete your data, see Delete your data. To withdraw your consent to ads, or object to them, use the game's Settings; to stop ads being linked to your phone, reset or delete its advertising ID in the phone's settings. To stop sending play data, switch off Share play data in Settings. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the CNPD (cnpd.pt), or the one where you live.

Children

ReVive is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you think a child has used the game in a way that sent us data, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
