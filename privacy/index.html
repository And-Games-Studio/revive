ReVive

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: ReVive is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts. Your town and your progress stay on your phone. The game sends nothing about how you play unless you say yes to "Help us improve" the first time you play; then it sends anonymous play data (which rooms you visit, how long steps take, what you decorate) to Unity Analytics, so we can make the game better. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Share play data. The game doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who we are

ReVive is made by Andrea Ebano, publishing as And Games, based in Portugal. Andrea Ebano is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com.

What data the game uses, and why

Playing

Your town's name, your progress (the places you've restored, down to each object), your decorations, coins, daily tasks and gifts, your album's photos and your settings are stored on your phone only. They are never sent anywhere.

Play data (Unity Analytics), only if you say yes

The first time you play, the game asks whether you want to help us improve it. If you say yes, it sends Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, events about how you play:

  • the places you enter and leave, how long you stay and how many objects you finish;
  • each restoration step you complete and how long it took, the objects and places you finish, and when you use a hint or follow a tip of the first-time guide;
  • the decorations you place and buy with in-game coins, the theme packs you open, the rare decorations you win and the daily tasks you claim;
  • which tabs you open, and that you shared a photo (not the photo);
  • the game's frame rate in each place.

With these, Unity's software sends a random identifier it creates for this installation of the game, when you play (sessions), your phone's model, operating system and language, the game's version, and your IP address, from which Unity works out your country. These events contain only identifiers and numbers: never your town's name, nothing you type, and nothing that tells us who you are. We use them to see where players get stuck or give up, and what to improve. If you say no, or never answer, nothing is sent. If you switch Share play data off later, sending stops at once.

Sharing a photo

"Share" in the album passes the before and after picture of a place, with a short text, to the app you pick. We don't see whom you send it to.

Technical data

The Unity engine, which the game is made with, may send Unity anonymous information about your device (model, operating system, graphics hardware) when the game starts.

Legal basis

Play data is sent only with your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Share play data.

How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Play data is kept in our Unity Analytics project only as long as we need it to improve the game, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com. To stop sending play data, switch off Share play data in the game's Settings. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Portugal, the CNPD (cnpd.pt), or the one where you live.

Children

ReVive is meant for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you think a child has shared play data, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
