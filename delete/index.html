ReVive

Delete your data

ReVive by And Games has no accounts of its own: your town and your progress live on your phone, and in your Google account if you signed in to Google Play Games. Here is how to delete what the game keeps, and what it sent.

In the game

Open ReVive, go to Settings and switch off Share play data. The game stops sending play data at once and asks Unity Analytics to delete the play data it sent from your phone.

To delete everything on your phone too, uninstall ReVive, or clear its data in your phone's settings (Apps → ReVive → Storage → Clear data). Your progress can't be recovered afterwards.

Don't have the game anymore, or want anything else deleted? Email ebano.andrea.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • The play data sent to Unity Analytics from your phone, if you had said yes to sharing it.
  • Everything the game keeps on your phone, when you uninstall it or clear its data: your town, progress, decorations, coins, album photos and settings.

What stays

  • Ad data held by Google: to stop ads being linked to your phone, reset or delete its advertising ID in the phone's settings (Google → Ads). Google keeps ad data as its privacy policy describes.
  • Progress saved in Google Play Games, if you signed in: delete ReVive's saved data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games app.
  • Your purchases, which Google Play records with your Google account: you can see them in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history.

Details are in our privacy policy.
